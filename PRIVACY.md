# Privacy Policy

**GerdQuest: Idle Depths** · Last updated: 2026-05-25

---

## Who we are

GerdQuest: Idle Depths is a free browser-based idle RPG made and operated by Todd Gerdy. You can reach me at **toadimous@gmail.com** with any privacy-related questions.

---

## What data is collected and why

### 1. Email address
When you sign in with Google, your email address is used as your account identifier in AWS Cognito. It is never displayed publicly or shared with other players.

**Why:** To uniquely identify your account so your save can be stored and retrieved.

### 2. Game save data
Your game state — characters, levels, inventory, gold, upgrades, settings, and other progress — is stored in a database linked to your account.

**Why:** To sync your save across devices and browsers.

### 3. Session metadata
Each save write stores a session identifier and timestamp to prevent conflicting saves from multiple browser tabs. This data has no meaning outside the game.

**Why:** To keep your save consistent if you play in multiple windows.

---

## What is NOT collected

- No passwords (sign-in is handled entirely by Google)
- No payment information (the game is free)
- No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers
- No location data
- No device fingerprinting

## Data retention

Your save data is kept for as long as your account exists. If you want your data deleted, email **toadimous@gmail.com** and I will remove it within 30 days.

---

## Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you can:

- **Access** — request a copy of the data stored for your account
- **Delete** — request deletion of your account and all associated data
- **Export** — the game's built-in export feature lets you download your save at any time

EU residents have additional rights under GDPR (rectification, restriction of processing, portability). California residents have rights under CCPA. To exercise any of these, contact **toadimous@gmail.com**.

The legal basis for processing your data is **legitimate interest** — storing only what is strictly necessary to provide the service you signed up for.

---

## Children

This game is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact me and I will delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, I will update the date at the top and note it in the game changelog.
